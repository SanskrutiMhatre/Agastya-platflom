Main hydraulic pump - Remove procedure

1Preliminary requirements

Support equipmentPart numberQty
Pump removal toolTL-29-1031
Blanking plug set, hydraulicBP-29-2101
WARNINGHydraulic fluid under pressure can cause injury. Release the pressure in the system before you disconnect any hose.

2Procedure

1
Put the aircraft in the maintenance configuration. Refer to DMC-…-00-00A-040A-A.
2
Release the pressure in the hydraulic system:
  1. Set the HYD PRESS switch to OFF.
  2. Operate the brake pedals until the accumulator pressure gauge shows 0 bar.
CAUTIONDo not let fluid fall on the electrical connectors. Use the blanking plugs.
3
Disconnect the pressure hose (5) from the pump (3) and install the blanking plugs. See Fig 1.
4
Remove the four bolts (4) and the pump (3) from the gearbox.
Figure 1 - Main hydraulic pump installation (ICN-AGSTY-A-291000-A-00A01-A-001-01)
NOTEKeep the removed bolts. They are not replaced at installation.
