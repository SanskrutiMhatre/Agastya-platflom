=====================================================================================================================
  THIS IS A SKELETON, NOT A LICENCE.  It is not shipped, and the installer does not read it.

  It lists the headings an end-user licence for this product usually has, with the points that are specific to
  Agastya Platform filled in as FACTS to be checked, and every legal decision left as a [PLACEHOLDER].
  Your organisation's legal adviser must write or approve the real text.

  To use it: have the licence text written, save it as  docs\EULA.txt  (plain text), and rebuild. The installer
  then shows a licence page that the user must accept. Without docs\EULA.txt the installer shows no licence page.
=====================================================================================================================
